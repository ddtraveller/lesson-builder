## CEFR A1-A2 Curriculum for Ages 10-12

### Recommended Topics (from Cambridge/Oxford wordlists)
- School life, hobbies & technology, future dreams, travel & nature
- A1: basic vocabulary (200-500 words), simple sentences
- A2: expanded vocabulary (500-1000 words), compound sentences, past tense

### Key Grammar Structures (A1-A2 progression)
1. Present Simple — daily routines, habits, facts
2. Present Continuous — actions happening now
3. Past Simple — narrating events, weekends, holidays
4. Comparatives & Superlatives — comparing things
5. Modal verbs — can/could/should/must for ability, permission, advice
6. Future — going to, will, want to be
7. Countable/Uncountable nouns — some, any, much, many
8. First Conditional — if + present, will + base verb
9. Imperatives — instructions, recipes, advice
10. Prepositions of movement — to, from, by, through

## Thai L1 Interference

### Pronunciation Difficulties
| English Sound | Thai Difficulty | Example |
|--------------|----------------|---------|
| /r/ vs /l/ | Interchanged | "read" → "lead" |
| /v/ vs /w/ | No /v/ in Thai | "vest" → "west" |
| /θ/ (th) | No equivalent | "think" → "sink"/"tink" |
| Final consonants | Dropped/simplified | "cats" → "cat", "played" → "play" |
| Consonant clusters | Simplified | "street" → "seet" |
| /ʃ/ (sh) | Often becomes /ch/ | "ship" → "chip" |

### Common Grammar Errors
1. **Missing "to be"** — "I student" instead of "I am a student"
2. **No plural -s** — "three cat" instead of "three cats"
3. **No 3rd person -s** — "He play" instead of "He plays"
4. **Tense confusion** — Using base form for all tenses (Thai has no conjugation)
5. **Article omission** — "I have dog" instead of "I have a dog"
6. **Word order** — Adjective after noun (Thai pattern) vs before noun (English)

### Teaching Implications for This Course
- Include explicit pronunciation practice in song pages (lyrics with audio)
- Stories should model correct grammar naturally
- Quiz questions should test common error patterns (plural -s, verb tense)
- Reading passages should highlight grammar patterns in context
- Game scramble/spelling exercises reinforce final consonants and spelling
